podup 1.9.1 is out. One thing worth calling out:

- The .deb package could previously carry a stale version number instead of the one actually released. It now ships the released version, and CI gates the build on that being correct before it publishes.

Full changelog: [v1.9.0...v1.9.1](https://github.com/Glyndor/podup/compare/v1.9.0...v1.9.1).
